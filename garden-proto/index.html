Sensor test

This checks whether your phone can tell where its camera is pointing. You need a clear view of the sun.

  1. Tap Start. Allow motion, location, and camera when asked.
  2. Follow the green box at the top of the screen. It tells you what to do at each step.
  3. Never look at the sun with your eyes. Use the screen.

Estimated. Calibrate the compass by moving the phone in a figure eight if the numbers drift.
